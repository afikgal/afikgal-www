Confidential16

Real-time stop-loss coordination aids cash-pay

Stop-loss rates are rising rapidly, driven by the leveraged trend effect for large claims above attachment points and compounded by a recent market pricing correction. Cash payments through single-case negotiations based on interim bills have been shown to drastically reduce costs.

  1. We risk-stratify cases before cost accumulates. Interim bills are requested for ongoing cases.
  2. The stop-loss carrier is notified before arriving at the attachment point and gets the full case and case review data.
  3. The interim bills are paid on an ongoing basis.
